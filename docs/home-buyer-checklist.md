\# Home Buyer Decision Checklist

\#\# BUDGET & FINANCING  
• Price within budget (overall quantum, monthly payments)  
• Loan eligibility and financing flexibility  
• Future affordability (interest rates, job stability, backup plan)

\#\# LOCATION & ACCESSIBILITY  
• Location and accessibility to work  
• Proximity to MRT/bus stops  
• Proximity to parents/family  
• Travel time to key daily places (work, school, childcare)

\#\# NEARBY AMENITIES  
• Nearby amenities (supermarkets, malls, food options)  
• Schools and childcare centres  
• Clinics/hospitals  
• Parks and green spaces

\#\# NEIGHBORHOOD & COMMUNITY  
• Overall neighborhood vibe and safety  
• Noise levels (roads, neighbors, facilities)  
• Cleanliness and upkeep of the estate  
• Demographics and community feel (families, tenants, etc.)

\#\# UNIT SIZE & LAYOUT  
• Size of the unit (sqft / sqm)  
• Practical and efficient layout  
• Number of bedrooms and bathrooms  
• Storage space

\#\# UNIT POSITIONING  
• Floor level (low/mid/high)  
• Facing (avoid direct west sun, prefer wind, views)  
• Privacy (distance to opposite blocks, corridor traffic)  
• Natural light and ventilation

\#\# PROPERTY CONDITION  
• Age and condition of the property  
• Move-in ready vs renovation needed  
• Quality of construction and finishes  
• Maintenance of common areas

\#\# FACILITIES  
• Condo / estate facilities (pool, gym, BBQ, playground, function rooms)  
• Quality and maintenance of facilities  
• Crowdedness of facilities

\#\# INVESTMENT POTENTIAL  
• Current rental demand in the area  
• Potential rental yield  
• Future resale demand and exit options  
• Future development plans (URA Master Plan, upcoming MRT, malls, etc.)

\#\# OTHER RULES & LOGISTICS  
• Rules and regulations (MCST, HDB rules)  
• Parking availability and costs  
• Pet-friendliness (if relevant)

\#\# FUTURE-PROOFING  
• Fit with life plans (marriage, kids, parents moving in)  
• Ability to age in place (lifts, accessibility)  
• Flexibility to reconfigure rooms in future
